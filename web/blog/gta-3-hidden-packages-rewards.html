Why packages matter

GTA III contains 100 hidden packages around Liberty City. Each package gives cash, while every ten packages unlocks a weapon or item pickup at safehouses. The system turns exploration into a permanent gameplay advantage.

Rewards from 10 to 50

At 10 packages you unlock the pistol, at 20 the Uzi, at 30 grenades, at 40 the shotgun and at 50 body armor. These milestones reduce the need to search for basic equipment before missions.

Rewards from 60 to 100

Later milestones unlock Molotov cocktails at 60, AK-47 at 70, sniper rifle at 80, M16 at 90 and the rocket launcher at 100. Completing the full set also gives a major cash reward.

Collect by island

Group packages by Portland, Staunton Island and Shoreside Vale instead of chasing isolated markers across the city. Some early-island packages are easier after later access opens.

Avoid losing track

Use a numbered checklist and mark each pickup immediately. GTA III does not provide a modern collectible tracker, so losing one package can create a long search near the end of a completion run.

Best milestones

For casual play, 20 and 50 are practical early goals. Completionists should continue to all 100 because hidden packages are required for 100% completion and the final reward is one of the strongest safehouse unlocks.
