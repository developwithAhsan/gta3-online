Portland is the opening island

Portland is the industrial first section of Liberty City and the only main island open at the beginning. Its streets introduce driving, wanted levels, weapons, gangs and the mission structure used throughout GTA III.

Major districts

Important areas include the Red Light District, Saint Mark's, Chinatown, Trenton, Portland Harbor, Harwood, Hepburn Heights and Atlantic Quays. Learning the roads between them saves time because early missions repeatedly reuse these corridors.

Early mission flow

Luigi introduces basic jobs, Joey expands into vehicle-focused missions and Toni Cipriani moves the story deeper into Leone family work. Salvatore eventually pushes progression toward the next island.

Useful early activities

Portland is a good place to learn taxi, firefighter, vigilante and paramedic routes while also collecting hidden packages. Do not worry if every package is not reachable normally at the very start.

Preparing for Staunton

Before leaving Portland, finish missable mission strands, build cash, learn the Pay 'n' Spray and Ammu-Nation locations and keep a backup save.

Portland in the browser build

The island map is original GTA III content; the browser layer changes how the engine loads and runs. Missing geometry after startup usually points to asset compatibility rather than a different Portland map.
