Why missions can be missed

GTA III allows several mission strands to be active at once. Story events can remove a mission giver or advance the world in ways that close earlier jobs. The story may still be finishable, but a 100% run can be damaged.

Portland safety rule

Finish available Luigi and Joey missions before advancing too far with Salvatore Leone. Completion guides commonly use Last Requests as the safety checkpoint because earlier mission strands can become inaccessible after story progression.

Staunton safety rule

Complete Kenji Kasen's available work before progressing Donald Love through the mission events that remove Kenji as a contact. Keep a manual save before major Donald Love missions.

Two-Faced Tanner timing

Completion guides also warn players to finish Two-Faced Tanner before Grand Theft Aero. The broader rule is simple: clear open side contacts before pushing a major story chain forward.

Use multiple saves

Rotate several save slots: current progress, a pre-mission-giver checkpoint and an older island checkpoint. A completion run deserves redundancy, especially when local browser data can be cleared separately from the game.

100% strategy

Do phone missions and side content as each island opens, collect packages while travelling and avoid rushing only the main story. Use the missions-in-order and 100% checklist guides together.
