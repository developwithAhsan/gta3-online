GTA III Missions and Progression Guide

Updated 20266 min read

GTA III gradually opens Liberty City as Claude works for different criminal organizations. The broad progression moves from Portland to Staunton Island and then to Shoreside Vale, with side missions and optional activities available between story jobs.

Portland: learning the city

Portland introduces driving, basic combat and the first major contacts. Early missions are the best time to learn shortcuts, weapon pickups, Pay 'n' Spray locations and how quickly the police response escalates.

Staunton Island: larger jobs and heavier resistance

Once Staunton Island becomes available, mission design becomes more complex and traffic routes matter more. Keep several saves and learn bridge approaches before timed missions.

Shoreside Vale: late-game routes

Shoreside Vale contains long roads, steep terrain and the airport area. For late missions, vehicle choice and knowing the tunnel/bridge network can save significant time.

Side activities worth doing

  • Taxi, police, ambulance and fire-service activities can reward long-term progression.
  • Hidden packages unlock weapon pickups at safehouses as milestones are reached.
  • Rampages and stunt jumps add optional completion goals and help you learn the map.
Tip: keep multiple save slots before major story transitions. GTA III is an older mission-driven game and a separate backup save makes experimentation much easier.
