GTA III Liberty City Map Guide

Updated 20266 min read

GTA III's Liberty City is split into three major areas that open as the story progresses. Each area has a different road layout, mission style and set of useful landmarks.

Portland

Portland is industrial and dense. It includes docks, elevated roads, narrow streets and many early mission locations. Learn the main north-south routes and the roads around the safehouse, Pay 'n' Spray and bridges.

Staunton Island

Staunton is more vertical and business-like, with broad roads, construction areas and major civic districts. It is easier to drive fast here, but traffic and sharp turns can still ruin timed objectives.

Shoreside Vale

Shoreside Vale has hills, suburban roads, the airport and longer travel distances. Route planning matters because a wrong turn can add a large detour.

Navigation habits

  • Memorize bridge and tunnel connections first.
  • Use large landmarks to orient yourself after respawning.
  • Learn at least two routes to each safehouse.
  • Before timed missions, drive the destination route once without pressure.
