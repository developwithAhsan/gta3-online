GTA III Hidden Packages Guide

Updated 20266 min read

Liberty City contains 100 hidden packages. They are small collectible pickups placed on rooftops, behind buildings, near infrastructure and in areas that reward careful exploration.

Why packages are useful

Package milestones unlock weapon pickups at your safehouse. That means collecting packages is not only a completion challenge; it can make later missions much more convenient because useful equipment becomes available close to your save point.

Collect by district

The easiest strategy is to finish one island at a time instead of collecting randomly. Divide your route into Portland, Staunton Island and Shoreside Vale, then mark each pickup as you collect it.

Bring the right vehicle

  • Use a nimble car for dense Portland streets.
  • Keep a fast vehicle for long Staunton routes.
  • For awkward locations, approach slowly and check alleys, ramps and raised areas.

Do not rely only on memory

Because there are 100 packages, missing a single one near the end can be frustrating. Use a checklist and record each pickup immediately. If you are aiming for 100% completion, this is far faster than revisiting the entire city later.
