Privacy

The GTA III browser launcher stores game-related data locally in your browser, including the optional OPFS archive cache and browser save data. These local files are controlled by your browser and can be removed using site-data controls or the launcher cache tools.

The site may be hosted through third-party infrastructure such as GitHub Pages and an asset proxy. Those providers may process ordinary request metadata according to their own policies. This project does not require you to submit account credentials to play.

Do not upload or use game data that you are not authorized to use.
