Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026.

Local browser storage

The launcher may store game-related data locally in your browser, including a compressed archive in Origin Private File System (OPFS), preferences such as FPS or touch-control mode, and browser-side save data where supported. These files remain associated with this site origin and can be removed through browser site-data controls or the site's cache controls.

Network requests

The site is hosted through GitHub Pages and may use an asset proxy for authorized game-data transfers. Hosting and proxy providers can process ordinary technical request information such as IP address, user agent, timestamps and requested URLs according to their own policies and infrastructure requirements.

Accounts and credentials

The project does not require a GTA III Browser account and does not ask users to submit game-store passwords or unrelated account credentials.

Third-party links

Articles may link to external resources. Their privacy practices are controlled by those sites, not by this project.

Your controls

You can clear this site's stored data through your browser settings. Removing site data can also remove cached game files and locally stored preferences.
